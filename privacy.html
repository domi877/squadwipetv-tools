Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how SquadWipeTV Tools (“the App”, “we”) handles data when it is connected to TikTok and Instagram accounts. The App is a private, non-public tool operated by Dominik Iffland (contact below) to manage the operator's own social media accounts (SquadWipeTV). It is not offered to the general public.

1. Controller

Dominik Iffland
E-mail: domiiffland@yahoo.de

2. What data the App accesses

Only after the account owner has explicitly authorized the App via the official TikTok Login Kit or Meta/Instagram login, the App may access:

  • Basic profile information – e.g. account ID, username, display name, avatar.
  • Account statistics – e.g. follower count, following count, number of likes and videos.
  • Video information and statistics – e.g. video IDs, titles/captions, creation time, links, view, like, comment and share counts of the authorized account's own videos.
  • Video upload – the App uploads video files created by the account owner to the account owner's own TikTok inbox (as drafts) or Instagram account.

The App does not access private messages, contacts, or data of other users, and does not collect data from viewers of the videos.

3. Purpose and legal basis

Data is processed solely to (a) publish or prepare content on the operator's own accounts and (b) analyze the performance of that content. The legal basis is the consent given during authorization and the operator's legitimate interest in managing their own accounts (Art. 6(1)(a) and (f) GDPR).

4. Storage and security

Access tokens and retrieved statistics are stored only locally on the operator's computer. They are not stored on any external server operated by us, are not sold, rented or shared with third parties, and are not used for advertising. Data is transmitted only to the respective platform (TikTok, Meta) via their official, encrypted APIs.

5. Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept as long as needed for content analysis. Access can be revoked at any time:

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions, remove the App.
  • Instagram: Settings → Website permissions / Apps and websites, remove the App.

After revocation, the App can no longer access the account. Locally stored data is deleted on request.

6. Third-party platforms

Use of TikTok and Instagram is additionally governed by their own terms and privacy policies: TikTok Privacy Policy, Instagram/Meta Privacy Policy.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. Contact us at the e-mail address above.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version is always available on this page.
